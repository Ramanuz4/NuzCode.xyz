Description :

Quick Draw Katana is a short reflex-based game inspired by classic samurai duels. Players must stay focused, wait for the right moment, and draw their katana with perfect timing to defeat their opponent. Acting too early or too late results in failure, making every duel a test of patience and reaction speed.
