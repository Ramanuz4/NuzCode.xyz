Description :

Welcome to Kitchen Chaos! 🔥 A fast-paced, fun-filled cooking game where you manage a busy kitchen, prepare dishes, and serve customers before they lose patience! 🏃‍♂️💨

Documentation :

Kitchen Chaos is a cooking simulation game where players take on the role of a chef in a bustling kitchen. The objective is to prepare and serve dishes to customers as quickly as possible while managing time and resources effectively. Players must chop ingredients, cook dishes, and plate them for service, all while keeping an eye on customer patience levels. The game features various recipes, kitchen tools, and challenges to keep the gameplay engaging and fast-paced.

🎮 Features

Time-Based Challenges

: Cook and serve as fast as you can!

Multiple Recipes

: Prepare a variety of dishes with unique ingredients.

Cartoonish Art Style

: Vibrant and fun graphics.

Engaging Soundtrack

: Enjoy lively background music while you cook.

🕹️ How to Play

Take Orders

: Check what the customers want.

Cook the Food

: Use ingredients and follow the recipe.

Serve on Time

: Deliver the dish before the customer leaves.
