About the game

Manage a bustling kitchen by preparing and serving dishes as quickly as possible while balancing time and resources. Chop ingredients, cook dishes, plate orders, and keep an eye on customer patience.

Features

Gameplay

  • Time-based challenges: cook and serve as fast as you can.
  • Multiple recipes with unique ingredients.
  • Cartoonish art style and lively soundtrack.
  • Take orders, prepare food, and serve before customers leave.
