Quick Draw Katana

A short reflex-based game inspired by classic samurai duels. Stay focused, wait for the right moment, and draw your katana with perfect timing.

RELEASE / MARCH 21, 2026GENRE / 2D DUEL / REACTION GAMEENGINE / UNREAL ENGINE 5 · BLUEPRINTS
Play / Download ↗Source on GitHub ↗

About the game

Wait until the signal appears, then click the left mouse button as quickly as possible. Acting too early or too late means failure; each duel tests patience and reaction speed.

Features

Gameplay

  • Fast-paced samurai duels.
  • Reflex- and timing-based gameplay.
  • Randomized draw timing for every round.
  • One-hit victory and simple controls.
